# Zomato-Project
🍽️ Zomato Restaurants Expansion Analysis (Excel Project)

📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes Zomato’s global restaurant data to identify high-potential countries and cities for opening new restaurants.
The analysis focuses on competition, customer ratings, pricing, cuisines, and convenience features (online delivery & table booking) using Microsoft Excel.

The goal is to provide data-backed strategic recommendations for market expansion with lower competition and higher customer satisfaction.

🎯 Business Objectives

Identify countries with low competition & high customer ratings

Analyze restaurant openings trend over the years

Study price ranges, customer votes, and ratings

Recommend cities and cuisines for future expansion

Evaluate impact of online delivery & table booking on ratings

📊 Key Insights

India (91%) and USA (4.5%) dominate Zomato’s restaurant presence

2011 & 2018 recorded peak restaurant openings

Indonesia leads in customer engagement (highest votes)

Philippines, Turkey & Indonesia show high ratings with lower competition

Canada is a low-competition “blue ocean” market

Online delivery & table booking significantly improve ratings

No strong correlation between higher price and better ratings
